### Bonds — proposed design

- **Schema:** a `bond_holdings` table with these fields:
  - `isin`, `issuer_type` (government / corporate / municipal / savings), `currency`, `face_value`, `quantity`
  - `coupon_rate` (decimal), `coupon_freq` (0/1/2/4/12), `coupon_type` (fixed / floating / inflation / reinvest), `day_count`
  - `issue_date`, `maturity_date`, `purchase_clean_price_pct`, `purchase_fx_czk`, `clean_price_pct`, `price_date`
  - `redeemable_early`, `liquidity_tier`, and `unique(profile_id, isin)`
  - Coupons are recorded as `transactions` with `asset_class='bond'`.
- **`src/lib/bonds.ts`** (pure functions, each with tests): `couponSchedule`, `accruedInterest`, dirty value, `ytm` (reusing the `xirr` solver), Macaulay/modified duration, convexity. With no price, a bond is valued at amortised cost or par and labelled "at cost". Czech savings bonds (Dluhopisy Republiky) are valued at redemption value.
- **Wiring:**
  - Add `'bond'` to `AssetClass`, `totalsByClass`, labels, the emergency haircuts, the MC assumptions (≈1% real, σ 5%, ρ≈0.1 vs stocks) and a new `--c-bond` colour.
  - Tag bond ETFs in `asset_metadata` so they map to `bond`.
  - Add a `rates_bp` shock to scenarios (ΔP ≈ −D·Δy + ½C·Δy²).
  - Show coupons and maturities in the calendar and projections.
- **Czech specifics:** 15% tax on coupons; interest accrued at purchase (AÚV) goes into cost basis; the 3-year time test applies to bonds too.

**Other gaps:** the 100k CZK annual exemption and the post-2025 time-test cap; the crypto time test; splits and spin-offs; net rental yield; mortgage amortisation in FIRE and MC.

*These are accuracy and transparency findings, not tax or investment advice.*
